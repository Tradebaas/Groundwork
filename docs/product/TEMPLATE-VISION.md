## How this document is used

Every feature in `docs/work/` names, in one line, the strategy line or the vision sentence it
serves. A feature that serves none of them is not out of scope by accident: it means either the
feature is wrong or this document is out of date, and one of the two has to change before it is
built. Purpose outranks the technically convenient route.
